- Light workspace theme with navy accents and improved form styling.
- Conversation history on the left and suggested prompts in the chat workspace.
- Engineering tools, preliminary selection and section iteration switch from a vertical navigation rail on the right.
- Engineering outputs, 3D model and CAD editor switch from a vertical navigation rail on the right.
